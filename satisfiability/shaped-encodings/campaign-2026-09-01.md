Run 2026-09-01 evening on a rented 96-core box (RunPod A40 host, about 0.65 $ total),
so the laptop's own timed measurements ran undisturbed; raw logs in the job folder
`work/2026-09-01_shaped-encodings/out/pod-campaign-records.tgz` on the machine.

## What ran

250 shaped `matmul_3x3x3 --target 23` XNF instances from this branch's generator
(pairing under the derived 19x1 + 4x2 quota; zeroing at 0, 6, 12, 18, 25, 37, 50 %;
streamline seeds 1-45), filtered and searched with xnfsat (`--witness=1`, its native

| stage | result |
|---|---|

## What the deaths taught (the campaign's real yield)

**The cross-term channel.** A product that owns two odd entries has their alpha,
beta, gamma forced through the shared `q` and `c` variables, which forces *other*
triple terms on that product; a zeroing draw (or an exclusivity unit) that hits a
forced term refutes the formula by propagation alone. Four doubles per instance
make almost every draw die this way at any real zeroing rate, which is why the
zeroing x pairing cross kills 197 of 250 and plausibly why the source's own
generation was discard-and-retry. Two fixes follow:

1. **Zero only the free terms**: compute the pairing's propagation closure first
   and exclude the forced terms from the zeroing pool - algebra-aware zeroing,
   cheap to add to `streamlining.cpp`.
2. **Reject at birth**: a 5 s solver call per candidate separates dead from
   searching for pennies; the generator should loop seeds until a quota of
   searching instances exists (their discipline, mechanised).

## What the negative means, honestly

Devices 3 + 4 as published are **not sufficient** to reproduce solvable shaped
instances at this budget: the claim "shaping moves xnfsat from zero to found"
stands unproven, tested and negative for this device subset. The review already
names what the solvable originals carry beyond it: the SAT 2019 row/column-zero
streamliners, the "each even term occurs zero times or twice" occurrence
constraint, ~50 % neighborhood fixing from a known solution (device 6, which needs
a scheme from `flip_graph/` first), and survival filtering over far more than 45
candidates. Those are the campaign's next devices, in that order; the quotient
device is unaffected by any of this and still owed its orbit argument.

## The above-rank sweep (2026-09-02, small hours; corrected the same day): the walk rides density down to 27

The sweep hypothesis - Las Vegas owns the top of a descending k-sweep because
satisfiability is monotone in k and solutions are dense above the rank - tested at 60 s,
one seed, plain encodings. The xnfsat column is the 2026-09-02 re-run; see the
correction below for why the first one was void:

| question | kissat | xnfsat (native XNF) |
|---|---|---|
| matmul_3x3x3 at 30 | found, 0.05 s | found, 0.03 s |
| matmul_3x3x3 at 27 | found, 0.03 s | found, 1.4 s |
| matmul_3x3x3 at 25 | found, 38 s | nothing at 60 s |
| matmul_3x3x3 at 23 (no anchor) | nothing at 60 s | nothing at 60 s |
| cyclic_f2_7 at 14, 15, 16 | nothing at 60 s | nothing at 60 s |

Two lessons, one the opposite of what this section first said. The wall above a known
rank is sharp for both families (kissat 38 s at 25 and dark at 23; the walk 1.4 s at 27
and dark at 25), and **the walk does find where solutions are dense**: the sweep's top
is where a Las Vegas solver earns its keep, and what starves it is the approach to the
rank - consistent with the solution-density reading the encoding-knowledge review
sourced, not with a shape that blocks it at every k. The sweep strategy is back on the
plan for the top of the descent; the walk-favoring encoding is still owed for the last
steps. cyclic_f2_7 stays undecided three above its target at this cap.

### Correction (2026-09-02): the first xnfsat column was written by a missing binary

As first recorded, every xnfsat cell above said "nothing". Those runs never ran: the
worktree recreation of 2026-09-01 deleted the las-vegas `build/third_party/`, `timeout
<path>` wrote "failed to run command" into each log, and a driver that read silence as
no-find counted voids as negatives. The same void holds for 34 of the overnight closure
wave's 58 runs (17 of 29 survivors never searched; its honest record is 0 finds of 24
real runs over 12 survivors) and for the device-6 calibration's xnfsat column. Re-run
2026-09-02 on a rebuilt pinned xnfsat through `decide-rank-by-sat`, which probes the
binary and re-multiplies any find: the sweep column above flips at 30 and 27; device 6
reproduces its reading exactly (kissat control 0.362 s at 50 % against 0.34 s recorded,
xnfsat nothing at 60 s at 50/48/45 %); the 17 unsearched survivors are queued at the
wave's own protocol. The pod campaign is unaffected: 404 logs, zero failed-to-run.
The class of bug is the las-vegas parser's `s UNKNOWN` one again - a no built from
silence - and the lasting guard sits where the last one did: measure.py now refuses a
solver path that does not exist, and a raw `timeout` loop is never again the record of
a negative.
